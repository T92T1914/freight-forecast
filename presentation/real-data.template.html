Retained real-data evaluation

A real index, a harder baseline.

The monthly-refit Ridge model loses to last observation on the 72 final-test months. It beats the seasonal baseline overall, but that does not make it the strongest forecast in this comparison.

This is the national, seasonally adjusted BTS Freight Transportation Services Index. Its units are index points, with 2000 averaging 100. These are not shipment counts or a carrier's operational records.

Final test: January 2020 through December 2025

Fixed and monthly-refit policies were compared on 2010 to 2019 only. Monthly refit was selected there. This presentation reads the saved final-test result. It does not train a model or repeat the evaluation.
